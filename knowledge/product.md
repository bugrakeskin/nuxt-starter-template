# Nuxt starter product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers preparing an admitted project from this generic Nuxt source baseline.
The starter does not declare that project's end users or Customer identity.

## Product Purpose

Provide reusable framework, theme, auth and delivery foundations. Generic showcase
routes demonstrate patterns; they are not a Customer's operational product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

[Overview](overview.md) owns repository scope. [Foundation](foundation.md) and
[architecture](architecture.md) own technical boundaries. Provisioning, Customer
resolution, meeting integration and business workflows are outside this baseline.
Runtime declarations are not evidence of a deployed Customer service.

## Brand Commitments

[Design](design.md) owns generic semantic Nuxt UI styling and runtime sources.
A clone defines its own reviewed brand and visual direction; it does not inherit
Unfogy App product users, brick identity, fixed viewport or meeting exceptions.

## Product Principles

Preserve source-backed framework facts and explicit capability limits. Keep secrets
out of browser code. Add project-specific domain behavior only in the admitted clone.

## Initialization and provenance

Apply [initialization](initialization.md) after an authorized clone. Replace this
starter's product purpose, users, capabilities and brand constraints with reviewed
project facts. Preserve starter remote and source commit; do not invent unknown
facts or leave template identity as project truth. This is a useful starter concept,
not an empty product questionnaire.
